Privacy Policy — US Citizenship Test

Last updated: October 2026

US Citizenship Test ("the app") is an independent study tool for the USCIS naturalization civics test. This policy explains what data the app accesses and why. The short version: the app works fully offline, has no account system, and does not sell or share your study data with anyone.

Data stored on your device

Your profile information, study progress, practice test history, and settings are stored locally on your device only, using Android's standard local storage. This data is never transmitted to us — we don't operate any server that receives it, and we have no way to see it.

Microphone access (Recall Mode)

Recall Mode lets you answer questions by voice instead of typing. When you use this feature, the app requests microphone access and uses Android's built-in speech recognition to convert your spoken answer into text. Depending on your device and settings, this recognition may happen entirely on-device, or your device's operating system may send the audio to Google's servers for transcription (standard behavior of Android's system speech recognizer, not something this app controls or adds on top of). We do not record, store, or transmit this audio ourselves — once transcribed to text, only the resulting text is used by the app, locally.

Microphone access is only requested when you actively tap the microphone button, and only used for this feature.

Internet access

The app makes one type of network request: an optional, periodic check for updated congressional representative data (state officials, used for state-specific interview questions), fetched from a public file hosted on GitHub. This request contains no personal information — it's a plain file download. If it fails or you're offline, the app uses its built-in, bundled copy of this data instead, so the app's core functionality never depends on having internet access.

What we don't do

  • We don't require account creation or sign-in.
  • We don't collect, sell, or share your personal information with third parties.
  • We don't show ads or use third-party advertising/tracking SDKs.
  • We don't use analytics services that track you across apps or websites.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for adults studying for the U.S. naturalization civics test and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@uscitizenshiptestapp.example.

This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by USCIS or DHS. It is an independent study tool.
